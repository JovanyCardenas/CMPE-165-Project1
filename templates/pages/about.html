About Expense Tracker

A simple personal finance application designed to make tracking everyday income, expenses, and budgets easier.

Our Purpose

Make spending easier to understand.

Keeping track of personal finances can become difficult when purchases, bills, subscriptions, and income are spread across different places.

Expense Tracker brings that information together so users can quickly record transactions and get a clearer picture of where their money is going.

Expense Tracker helps you:

✓

Record income and expenses in one place.

✓

Organize spending using categories.

✓

Set budgets and monitor spending progress.

✓

View useful summaries of financial activity.

Built around simplicity

The application focuses on the essentials of everyday expense tracking.

⚡

Simple

Financial information should be quick to enter and easy to understand.

📊

Useful

Summaries and budgets turn transaction data into useful financial information.

🔒

Personal

Each user's financial information remains associated with their own account.

SJSU CMPE 165 Project

Expense Tracker is being developed as a software product prototype for CMPE 165 — Software Engineering Process Management at San José State University.
